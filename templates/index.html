📚 Voting Laws & Education

⚖️

Right to Vote

Article 326 of the Constitution grants universal adult suffrage. Every citizen above 18 years has the right to vote, irrespective of caste, religion, or gender.

📝

Registration Laws

The Representation of the People Act, 1950, mandates that a person must be an ordinary resident in a constituency to be registered as a voter there.

🕵️

Secret Ballot

Voting in India is conducted via Secret Ballot using EVMs (Electronic Voting Machines), ensuring that a voter's choice remains completely private and anonymous.

🚫

NOTA

None of the Above (NOTA) allows voters to officially reject all candidates in an election, expressing their dissatisfaction with the available options.

📜

Model Code of Conduct

A set of guidelines issued by the ECI to regulate the conduct of political parties and candidates during elections, ensuring fair play and order.

🔄

Anti-Defection Law

The 10th Schedule of the Constitution prevents elected members from switching political parties after elections to maintain political stability.

💰

Expenditure Limits

The ECI sets strict limits on the amount a candidate can spend on their election campaign to prevent the influence of excessive money power.

🖨️

VVPAT Verification

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail (VVPAT) allows voters to verify that their vote was cast correctly by viewing a printed slip through a glass window.

🏛️ Political Parties & Mottos

National Parties

🪷

Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)

Motto/Ideology: Hindutva, Integral Humanism.

Focuses on national security, economic growth, and cultural nationalism.

✋

Indian National Congress (INC)

Motto/Ideology: Secularism, Social Democracy.

Emphasizes social welfare, secular values, and inclusive growth.

🧹

Aam Aadmi Party (AAP)

Motto/Ideology: Anti-corruption, Welfarism.

Focuses on accessible healthcare, education, and rooting out corruption.

🐘

Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP)

Motto/Ideology: Social Equality, Ambedkarism.

Aims to represent marginalized sections of society, including Dalits, Adivasis, and OBCs.

🌾

Communist Party of India (M)

Motto/Ideology: Marxism, Communism.

Focuses on workers' rights, land reform, and anti-capitalism.
